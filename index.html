Jollof Rice

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato sauce.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 6 people

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of rice
  • 1kg Chicken/beef
  • 1 Teaspoon Curry Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Thyme
  • 1000ml of Ground fresh tomatoes
  • 50ml Ground Fresh peppers
  • 1 Nutmeg powder
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 cup of sliced onion
  • 300ml vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 fingers of ginger
  • Vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken/beef and let it sit.
  2. Boil for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Set aside meat and stock.
  4. Parboil and wash rice.
  5. Deep-fry meat and set aside.
  6. Fry tomatoes for about 20 minutes.
  7. Add stock, spices, and water.
  8. Add rice and cook on low heat.

Tips / Notes

Use long-grain parboiled rice for best results.

Avoid stirring too often to prevent mushy rice.

Let the rice steam on low heat for a smoky flavor.
