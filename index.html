Jollof Rice

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato sauce.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Servings: 4 people

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 1kg Chicken/beef
  • 1 Teaspoon Curry Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Thyme
  • 1000ml of Ground fresh tomatoes
  • 50mlGround Fresh peppers
  • 1 Nutmeg powder
  • 2 seasoning cube
  • I cupof sliced onion
  • 30oml vegetable
  • salt to taste
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 finger of Ginger
  • Vegetable oil

Instructions

  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers, and onions.
  2. Cook the blended mixture until thick.
  3. Add chicken stock and spices.
  4. Add rice and stir well.
  5. Cook until rice is soft and fully done.

Tips / Notes

Use long-grain parboiled rice for best results.

Avoid stirring too often to prevent mushy rice.

Let the rice steam on low heat for a smoky flavor.
