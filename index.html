Jollof Rice

A flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato sauce.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Cook Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 6 people

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of rice
  • 1kg Chicken/beef
  • 1 Teaspoon Curry Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Thyme
  • 1000ml of Ground fresh tomatoes
  • 50mlGround Fresh peppers
  • 1 Nutmeg powder
  • 2 seasoning cube
  • I cup of sliced onion
  • 300ml vegetable
  • salt to taste
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 finger of Ginger
  • Vegetable oil
Just wash, squeeze out water inside the tomatoes, blend as is, and set aside

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken/beef with a teaspoon of salt, one seasoning cubes, half a teaspoon each of curry and thyme, half a cup of sliced onions then let it sit for some minutes.

    2. Set on heat and let it boil for 10-15 minutes, would take more time to cook the beef.

  2. Set aside the meat and the stock in fresh bowls.

    3. Peel off the outer back of the ginger/garlic, pound, or grind.

  3. Parboil rice the normal way, boil about 7 cups of water in a pot, add the four cups of rice and allow boiling for 3 minutes. Then pour out and wash thoroughly with clean water, then set aside in a bowl or plastic sieve.

  4. Pick out the parboiled tasty meat and deep-fry, set aside

  5. Once you are done with frying the chicken, reduce the oil to 250ml (1 cup) just add the remaining one cup of sliced onions, stir for 1-2 minutes, add the ground tomatoes. I like to cook jollof rice with the same oil I used while frying the meat.


    6. Using a fresh oil is just the same as cooking without the beef stock (meat water) which actually holds over 70 percent of the ingredient that is used in making most Nigerian foods.

    The end result is not the same

  6. Frying the tomatoes would take about 20 minutes. Fry until when you lift a portion with your frying spoon, you will notice the oil continues to boil in the spoon.
    7. You also notice the tomatoes turning dark.

  7. Once the tomato is well fried, add the meat water (chicken/beef stock) to the fried tomato in the pot, the ground ginger/garlic, half a teaspoon each of curry and thyme, salt to taste, add 4 cups of water. Add another seasoning cube. Stir and taste, it should taste overly spiced. Don't worry, once the rice is added the taste would balance

  8. Add the rice.Then cover and allow the rice to cook on low heat for 30-45 minutes. Remember that you can add water occasionally.

    Voila!!!

    Turn and serve with the fried meat.

Tips / Notes

Use long-grain parboiled rice for best results.

Avoid stirring too often to prevent mushy rice.

Let the rice steam on low heat for a smoky flavor.
